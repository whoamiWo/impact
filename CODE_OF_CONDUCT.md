# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1. The full text is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/ and is incorporated by reference.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
chat channels, events) and when an individual officially represents the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spamming, brigading, or proselytizing in project spaces.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report incidents to whoami@wo.cn. Maintainers aim to acknowledge reports within 5 business days.
Reports are kept as private as legally possible. Maintainers with a conflict of interest recuse
themselves from handling the case.

## Enforcement

Enforcement follows an escalating ladder:

1. Private reminder or content edit.
2. Formal warning.
3. Temporary suspension from project spaces.
4. Permanent ban.

Severe violations (threats, doxxing, sustained harassment) may lead directly to a permanent ban.
Enforcement actions and their rationale are recorded by the maintainers.
